KIMA EIS — OPERATIONAL VISION APPLIED

PRODUCT VISION
For the engineer, control-room operator, and field technician, KIMA EIS should turn engineering information and measurements into traceable understanding, decision options, and safer next steps for successful operation and asset/information management.

IMPLEMENTED IN THIS BUILD
- Operational Decision Center.
- Role-aware views: Engineer / Control Room / Field.
- Measurement/Observation capture with unit and explicit threshold context.
- Deterministic decision engine.
- Decision persistence and audit events.
- Decision states: NORMAL / WATCH / CRITICAL / DATA_INSUFFICIENT.
- Evidence-aware confidence.
- Explicit safety boundary: no automatic control command.
- IndexedDB V7 decision store and migration by object-store creation.
- V7 backup support.
- Service-worker cache V7 includes decision.js.

DECISION QUALITY RULE
The system prefers an honest DATA_INSUFFICIENT result over an invented operational recommendation.

NEXT CONTROLLED EXPANSION
1. Trend engine over time-series observations.
2. Approved rule profiles per asset class/system.
3. QA findings feeding decision state.
4. Multi-signal correlation with deterministic rules.
5. P&ID/topology context on decision cards.
6. Change-impact linkage.
7. Procedures/SOP references and evidence.
8. Formal alarm/setpoint governance before any use in safety-critical contexts.
